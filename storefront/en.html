Frequently asked questions

Clarity before buying.

Answers about the integrated Hoosh Digital platform.

Where are invoices and payments handled?

WHMCS manages the billing flow. Payment gateways must be configured and tested directly within WHMCS.

Will hosting be provisioned automatically?

Only after product mapping, CyberPanel module setup and successful end-to-end testing. No unverified delivery claims are made.

How do I access my client account?

Client login currently uses WHMCS authentication. True single sign-on requires validated identity mapping.
